My research looks at how **public health insurance** and related programs protect people from major shocks such as **involuntary job loss** or **health shocks**, and who gets left behind. I use **large federal survey data** and **quasi-experimental methods** to study coverage, out-of-pocket costs, and premium burden, and I am keen to extend this to **health and wellbeing outcomes** such as mental health and unmet care.

<p>Unpublished manuscripts — not peer-reviewed journal publications.</p>
